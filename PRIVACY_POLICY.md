# Privacy Policy for Extreme Slo-Mo

**Effective date: September 30, 2026**

Extreme Slo-Mo respects your privacy. The app is designed to record and process slow-motion videos directly on your device.

## Data Collection

Extreme Slo-Mo does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data with the developer or third parties.

The app does not include advertising, analytics services, tracking technologies, user accounts, or a developer-operated online service.

## Camera and Microphone

Extreme Slo-Mo requests access to your device's camera to display a camera preview and record videos.

If audio recording is enabled, the app requests access to your microphone to include audio in the original recording. The processed slow-motion video is silent.

Camera and microphone content is processed locally on your device. It is not transmitted to the developer or to a developer-operated server.

## Photos and Files

Extreme Slo-Mo requests add-only access to your photo library so it can save videos that you create. Add-only access does not allow the app to browse or retrieve the existing contents of your photo library.

You may also choose a storage destination through Apple's system file picker. The app stores a local security-scoped bookmark so it can continue saving to your selected destination. If you select a folder managed by a cloud-storage provider, that provider may process the saved file under its own terms and privacy policy. The developer of Extreme Slo-Mo does not receive or have access to those files.

## On-Device Processing and Storage

Recorded videos and interpolated frames are processed locally on your device. Temporary files may be created during recording and processing. Completed videos are retained in the destination you select, such as your photo library or a selected folder.

App preferences—including recording, playback, aspect-ratio, processing-quality, and saving options—are stored locally on your device.

## Apple Watch Communication

If you use the companion Apple Watch app, Extreme Slo-Mo uses Apple's WatchConnectivity framework to exchange recording commands and basic recording-status information between your paired devices. This communication is used only to provide the remote-control feature and is not sent to the developer.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Because the developer does not collect or store personal data, there is no developer-held personal data to retain or delete.

You control your recordings and may delete them from Photos, Files, or the storage provider where you chose to save them. You can remove locally stored app preferences and temporary app data by deleting the app.

## Permissions and Your Choices

You can revoke Camera, Microphone, or Photos permission at any time through the Settings app on your device. Revoking a permission may prevent the related app feature from working.

## Children's Privacy

Extreme Slo-Mo does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
